# Account

Click your username in the menu bar, then **Usage & credits**.

Steps that run on the GPU use credits: detecting, tracking, identifying species, auto-labelling and
training. One credit is one GPU-hour of video analysis (about an hour of footage); SAM 3, sampling
and training use two an hour.

Every account starts with 25 credits. They don't expire, and they don't renew: when you need more,
ask from this page.

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **GPU credits** | What you have left, and what you used this month |
| **Your plan** | Standard or Pro, with buttons to request more credits, Pro, or longer storage of raw footage |
| **Your requests** | What you asked for and whether it was approved |
| **Lifetime stats** | Jobs submitted and completed, and total GPU time |
| **Plans** | What Standard and Pro include |
| **Have a promo code?** | Redeem a code for credits, and see the codes you've used |

Requests are reviewed by the BeeMonitor team, who reply by email. Nothing is paid on the platform.

## Raw footage

On Standard, raw footage is kept for 3 months after upload. Before any is deleted you get three
emails, 30, 15 and 7 days ahead, and a notice on this page with the date and how much. Everything
derived from it stays: detections, tracks, events, crops and annotations. A clip whose raw footage
was deleted can't be analysed again.

To keep footage longer, use **Request longer storage**. Nothing is deleted while a request is
waiting. On Pro, nothing is deleted.

To change your password, sign out and use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
