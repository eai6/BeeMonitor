# Browse

**Browse** lists datasets and models other people have published. Anyone can read and copy them, but only
the owner can change them.

- **Datasets**: **Take a copy** duplicates a project's frames, boxes and classes into a new
  [annotation project](annotations.md) you own, which records where it came from. Its card says whether
  recording times and sites were included.
- **Models**: a published model can be chosen in your own pipeline's **Detect** step. Its card records the
  dataset it was trained on and how large that dataset was at training time.

To publish your own, use **Publish** on an [annotation project](annotations.md#publishing-a-project) or a
[trained model](training.md#custom-models).
