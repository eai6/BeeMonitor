Off stops the camera recording entirely (telemetry keeps running). Motion-triggered saves a clip only when something moves (default); it closes 10 seconds after motion stops, and constant motion is capped at 10-minute clips. Clock / time

{% if metrics.ntp_synced %} NTP synced {% elif metrics.ntp_synced is False %} NTP not synced {% else %} NTP unknown {% endif %}

The device disciplines its clock to internet time (NTP) and copies it into the WittyPi RTC, which restores the clock on every wake before any network exists. Accurate time keeps the wake window and clip timestamps correct. Sync forces an immediate re-true on the device's next check-in.
