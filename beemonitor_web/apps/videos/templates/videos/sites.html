Sites

A site is a place you record without a unit. Its ROI and reference objects apply to every clip at the site, so you draw once for many clips. Units keep their layout on the unit's page.

Drawing for one clip? You don't need a site: open the clip and click Draw on its "ROI & reference objects" card.
