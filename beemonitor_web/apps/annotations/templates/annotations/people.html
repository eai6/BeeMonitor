What each role may do

ViewerSee frames, boxes and stats. Export the dataset. Change nothing. AnnotatorDraw and edit boxes, on the clips assigned to them — or that they take from the unassigned pool. ReviewerThe same, on anyone's clips. The checking role: someone who spots a bad box can fix it. ManagerAdd clips, sample, auto-label, edit the class list, assign work. OwnerAll of it, plus delete the project and manage this page.
